# GRAM TARANG 13 CENTRES & GEOGRAPHIC LOCATIONS MASTER DIRECTORY
# Official Detailed Guide: Infrastructure, Trades, Hostels, Lab Equipment & Contacts

Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) operates 13 specialized skill training centres across 5 Indian states (Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Jharkhand, and Assam). It utilizes an integrated Hub-and-Spoke model with Mother Centres attached to Centurion University campuses, supported by specialized industrial and community training centres in rural, tribal, and mining districts.
