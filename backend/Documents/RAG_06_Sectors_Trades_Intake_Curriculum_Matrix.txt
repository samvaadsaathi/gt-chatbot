# GRAM TARANG SECTORS, TRADES, CURRICULUM & INTAKE MASTER MATRIX
# Comprehensive Course Specifications, Syllabus Breakdowns, NSQF Levels, Tooling & Outcomes

Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) offers training across 11 major industry verticals and 40+ specialized job roles aligned with National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF). All curricula are co-designed with Sector Skill Councils (SSCs) and leading OEM manufacturers.

VERTICAL 1: ADVANCED MANUFACTURING, PRECISION ENGINEERING & CAPITAL GOODS
================================================================================

1. TRADE: CNC OPERATOR (TURNING & MILLING)
- NSQF Level: Level 4
- Duration: 3 Months (Full-Time, 480 Hours)
- Theory vs. Practical Ratio: 25% Theory : 75% Hands-on Machine Operating
- Minimum Eligibility: 10th Pass, 12th Pass, or ITI (Fitter/Turner/Machinist)
- Age Limit: 18 to 35 Years
- Certified By: Capital Goods Skill Council (CGSC) & NCVT
- Lab Machinery & Software:
  * Authentic Jyoti CNC 3-Axis Lathes & VMC (Vertical Machining Centers).
  * Fanuc 0i-TF and Siemens 828D/840D CNC control panels.
  * Digital Height Gauges, Micrometers, Vernier Calipers, Dial Indicators.
- Detailed Syllabus Modules:
  * Module 1 (Weeks 1-2): Workshop Safety, 5S Housekeeping, Engineering Drawing reading, GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing) symbols, Precision measurement tools.
  * Module 2 (Weeks 3-4): Machine construction, axes movement (X, Y, Z), cutting tools, inserts geometry (carbide, coated), coolant systems.
  * Module 3 (Weeks 5-8): G-Code and M-Code programming fundamentals, canned cycles (G71 turning, G76 threading, G81 drilling), work coordinate systems (G54-G59), tool geometry offsets and wear offsets.
  * Module 4 (Weeks 9-10): Part setup, vice/chuck truing, dry runs, single block execution, first-off inspection.
  * Module 5 (Weeks 11-12): Live production shift execution in Work-Integrated Learning (WEL) shop floor, cycle time optimization, statistical quality control.
- Placement Package: Rs. 14,000 to Rs. 22,000 / month + overtime + subsidized food/canteen.
- Key Recruiters: Ashok Leyland ancillaries, Tata Motors vendors, Bharat Forge, Godrej, SEDA, Sansera Engineering.

2. TRADE: CNC PROGRAMMER / CAD-CAM SPECIALIST
- NSQF Level: Level 5
- Duration: 4 Months (560 Hours)
- Eligibility: Diploma in Mechanical Engineering / ITI Machinist with 1 yr exp.
- Syllabus Highlights: Mastercam toolpath creation, multi-axis surface contouring, post-processor selection, tool library management, fixture design in SolidWorks.
- Placement Package: Rs. 18,000 to Rs. 28,000 / month.

3. TRADE: INDUSTRIAL FITTER
- NSQF Level: Level 3-4
- Duration: 3 Months (400 Hours)
- Eligibility: 8th / 10th Pass
- Syllabus Modules: Bench fitting, filing, hacksawing, scrapers, reamers, drilling, tap and die threading, pneumatic and hydraulic valves, mechanical maintenance, bearing assembly, shaft alignment, coupling installation.
- Placement Package: Rs. 13,000 to Rs. 18,000 / month.
- Recruiters: L&T Construction, Tata Steel contractors, heavy equipment manufacturers.

4. TRADE: MIG/MAG & TIG WELDER (GAS METAL ARC WELDING)
- NSQF Level: Level 4
- Duration: 3 Months (420 Hours)
- Equipment: Lincoln Electric MIG/MAG power sources, Argon purge TIG rigs, digital weld inspection tools.
- Syllabus Modules: Butt joints, lap joints, T-joints, pipe welding (1G to 4G positions), shielding gas mixing (Argon-CO2), weld defects analysis, non-destructive testing (NDT) basics.
- Placement Package: Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 24,000 / month (High international demand in Middle East).

VERTICAL 2: AUTOMOTIVE & HEAVY COMMERCIAL VEHICLE SERVICES
================================================================================

1. TRADE: ASHOK LEYLAND COMMERCIAL VEHICLE SERVICE TECHNICIAN
- NSQF Level: Level 4
- Duration: 4 Months (Residential, 600 Hours)
- Minimum Eligibility: ITI (Motor Mechanic / Diesel Mechanic / Fitter) or 10th Pass with mechanical aptitude
- Joint Certification: Automotive Skills Development Council (ASDC) & Ashok Leyland Ltd.
- Training Hubs: Central Mother Campus at Bhubaneswar & Regional Automotive Bays
- Authentic Equipment: Full-scale Ashok Leyland BS-VI chassis, CRS diesel engines, common-rail injectors, pneumatic brake rigs, i-Gen6 diagnostic scan tools.
- Detailed Syllabus Modules:
  * Module 1: BS-VI Engine architecture, CRDI fuel injection, SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction), DEF (Diesel Exhaust Fluid) doser systems, Turbochargers.
  * Module 2: Heavy Transmission systems, synchromesh gearboxes, clutch overhaul, propeller shafts, differential assemblies, axle hubs.
  * Module 3: Dual-circuit air brake systems, ABS (Anti-lock Braking), air dryers, compressor overhaul, pneumatic valve testing.
  * Module 4: Vehicle auto-electricals, 24V starter motors, heavy alternators, ECU sensor wiring, diagnostic trouble code (DTC) reading and troubleshooting.
  * Module 5: Customer service management, job card generation in DMS, workshop safety and SOP adherence.
- Placement Guarantee: 100% placement linkage across Ashok Leyland's network of 300+ authorized dealerships and service workshops across India.
- Starting Remuneration: Rs. 14,500 to Rs. 22,000 / month + incentive bonuses + uniforms.
