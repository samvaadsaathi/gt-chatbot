# GRAM TARANG CORPORATE PARTNERS, RECRUITERS & ALUMNI SUCCESS STORIES
# Comprehensive Guide to Industry Linkages, Joint Academies & Career Transformations

Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) does not follow a conventional training model where students are trained in isolation and then sent for generic job hunts. Instead, Gram Tarang operates on an inverted, employer-led model:
1. Long-term corporate memorandums of understanding (MoUs) are signed first.
2. The employer defines the skill gap, approves or provides the machinery, and co-designs the syllabus.
3. Master instructors are trained directly on the employer's factory floor.
4. Trainees practice on real industrial parts and diagnostic tools.
5. On certification, the employer hires the batch directly with guaranteed wage employment, statutory benefits (EPF, ESIC), and career growth ladders.

1. ASHOK LEYLAND (FLAGSHIP STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP)
- History: Strategic partnership initiated to address the acute shortage of trained, certified commercial vehicle technicians across Ashok Leyland's pan-India dealer network.
- Academy Setup: State-of-the-art Automotive Center of Excellence established at Gram Tarang Mother Campus (Bhubaneswar).
- Equipment Supplied by Ashok Leyland:
  * Full-chassis heavy commercial vehicle models.
  * BS-VI compliant diesel engine test beds with common-rail fuel injection.
  * Proprietary i-Gen6 diagnostic scan tools and electronic control modules (ECMs).
  * Pneumatic brake test rigs and heavy axle differential cut-sections.
- Training Format: 4-month residential boot camp with mandatory daily workshop shifts.
- Placement Linkage: 100% absorption across 300+ authorized Ashok Leyland dealerships and regional workshops across India (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Raipur, Bhubaneswar, Guwahati).
- Cumulative Outcome: Over 12,000+ commercial vehicle technicians trained and deployed.

2. TATA MOTORS
- Collaboration: Joint technician and apprentice training programs linked with Tata Motors dealerships and component vendors in Jamshedpur (Adityapur industrial cluster) and Pune (Chakan auto cluster).
- Training Focus: Passenger vehicle and commercial vehicle chassis repair, automotive electrical harness diagnosis, and precision component assembly.
- Career Path: Apprentices typically begin as Trainee Mechanics, progressing to Master Technicians and Diagnostic Specialists within 3 years.

3. YAMAHA MOTOR INDIA SALES PVT. LTD.
- Academy Setup: "Yamaha Training Academy" established inside Gram Tarang campuses.
- Facilities: 6 dedicated hydraulic two-wheeler service bays, genuine Yamaha special service tools (SSTs), fuel-injection diagnostic tools (YDT), and authentic 150cc–250cc motorcycle engines.
- Recruitment: Direct placement across Yamaha authorized dealerships in Eastern and Central India.

4. CAFÉ COFFEE DAY (AMALGAMATED BEAN COFFEE TRADING COMPANY)
- Pioneer Social Initiative: Co-created specialized barista and brewmaster curriculum for youth from tribal regions and youth with disabilities.
- Inclusive Silent Brewmasters Program: Gram Tarang and CCD pioneered a dedicated training track for Speech and Hearing Impaired (Deaf & Mute) candidates. Master trainers trained in Indian Sign Language (ISL) teach coffee recipes, espresso calibration, milk steaming, and order dispatch.
- Placement Record: Over 450+ speech-and-hearing impaired youth placed across CCD outlets in major metropolitan cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata).

5. GAP INC. P.A.C.E. & APPAREL EXPORT CONSORTIUM
- Program: Personal Advancement & Career Enhancement (P.A.C.E.) life-skills program integrated into technical sewing machine training.
- Recruiter Network: Shahi Exports (India's largest garment exporter), Gokaldas Exports, Arvind Mills, Raymond, Laguna Clothing.
- Scope: 52,628+ rural women trained and placed in organized garment manufacturing units, providing them with independent bank accounts, dormitory accommodation, and financial freedom.

6. SCHNEIDER ELECTRIC FOUNDATION
- Focus: Center of Excellence in Electrical & Solar Renewable Energy.
- Contribution: Schneider Electric donated cutting-edge industrial switchgear, motor control centers (MCC), miniature circuit breakers (MCBs), solar PV test rigs, and personal protective equipment (PPE).
- Outcomes: Hundreds of rural youth certified annually as licensed electricians and solar technicians.

7. GODREJ & BOYCE, VOLTAS & SEDA
- Precision tooling, domestic appliances servicing, refrigeration and air conditioning (HVAC) repair, and sheet metal fabrication apprenticeships.

SECTION 2: AUDITED PLACEMENT METRICS & STANDARDS
================================================================================
- Overall Placement Offer Rate: 78% to 82% verified across all batches.
- Formal Sector Social Security: 100% of placed trainees receive formal employment contracts with Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).
- Post-Placement Tracking Duration: 6 to 12 months. Gram Tarang maintains dedicated Post-Placement Tracking Officers (PPTO) who:
  * Help candidates find safe, affordable hostel accommodation in destination cities.
  * Assist with local SIM card procurement, opening bank accounts, and salary remittance home.
  * Mediate workplace grievances and provide emotional counseling to prevent homesickness and dropouts.
- Remittance Impact: Gram Tarang alumni remit an estimated average of Rs. 6,000 to Rs. 10,000 per month back to their rural households, funding siblings' education, healthcare, and building permanent pucca homes.

CASE STUDY 1: MANAS RANJAN NAYAK — FROM RURAL SCHOOL DROPOUT TO WORKSHOP MANAGER
- Background: Native of a remote agricultural village in Nayagarh district, Odisha. His father was a marginal farmer struggling under heavy seasonal debt. Manas dropped out of school after 10th grade due to financial constraints.
- Gram Tarang Intervention: Mobilized during a DDU-GKY village awareness camp and enrolled in the 4-month Ashok Leyland Commercial Vehicle Technician residential course at Bhubaneswar.
- Initial Training: Learnt engine diagnostics, BS-IV/BS-VI common rail systems, and air brake troubleshooting.
- First Job: Placed at an Ashok Leyland authorized dealership in Cuttack at a starting wage of Rs. 11,500/month.
- Career Growth: Promoted within 18 months to Senior Diagnostic Technician. Later earned certification as Master Technician.
- Current Status: Works as Workshop Floor Supervisor at a prominent commercial vehicle dealership in Bhubaneswar, earning Rs. 38,000/month, managing a team of 14 mechanics. His remittances completely cleared his family's debt and built a 3-room concrete house in his native village.

CASE STUDY 2: BASANTI MAJHI — BREAKING GENDER BARRIERS IN PRECISION CNC MACHINING
- Background: Belonging to a tribal community in Rayagada district, Odisha. In her community, girls were traditionally married early or restricted to seasonal agricultural labor.
- Gram Tarang Intervention: Supported under the Odisha Skill Development Authority (OSDA) "Sudakshya" scheme with a 100% fee waiver and free hostel. Enrolled in CNC Operator (Milling & Turning).
- Training Experience: Trained on authentic Jyoti 3-axis CNC machines and learned G-code programming, micrometer precision measurement down to 10 microns, and live production shifts.
- Placement: Recruited directly by Sansera Engineering (a leading precision automotive component manufacturer in Pune).
- Current Status: Works as Senior CNC Machine Operator in Pune, earning Rs. 24,500/month + performance bonuses. She sends Rs. 12,000 home every month, financing her younger brother's engineering college tuition.

CASE STUDY 3: DEEPAK KUMAR SAHOO — SILENT BREWMASTER AT CAFÉ COFFEE DAY
- Background: Born with profound hearing and speech impairment in Ganjam district, Odisha. Faced societal isolation and lack of formal employment opportunities.
- Gram Tarang Intervention: Joined the specialized Café Coffee Day "Silent Brewmaster" residential training program at Paralakhemundi.
- Training Experience: Taught espresso machine calibration, latte art, hygiene protocols, and billing using sign language and visual cue charts.
- Placement: Placed at a high-traffic Café Coffee Day outlet in Bengaluru.
- Current Status: Senior Barista at CCD MG Road, Bengaluru. Has won the "Customer Star of the Month" award three times for exceptional beverage quality and hospitality service. Earns Rs. 19,000/month with company accommodation and medical benefits.

CASE STUDY 4: SUNITA HEMBRAM — TRIBAL YOUTH EMPOWERMENT IN APPAREL EXPORTS
- Background: Santhal tribal woman from East Singhbhum, Jharkhand. Family had no regular income source.
- Gram Tarang Intervention: Enrolled in the Industrial Sewing Machine Operator (SMO) trade at the Jamshedpur center with integrated Gap Inc. P.A.C.E. life skills.
