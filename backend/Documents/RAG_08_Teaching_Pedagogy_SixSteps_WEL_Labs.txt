# GRAM TARANG 6-STEP TEACHING METHODOLOGY & WORK-INTEGRATED LEARNING (WEL)
# Deep Technical Guide to Vocational Pedagogy, Production Floors & Skill Ecosystem

Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) pioneered a distinctive, world-recognized vocational training pedagogy designed specifically to bridge the immense gap between underprivileged rural school dropouts and the high-precision demands of modern manufacturing and service industries.

================================================================================
SECTION 1: THE CORE PEDAGOGICAL PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================
Core Motto:
"Nobody touches a production machine before they have watched the job done. Nobody is certified by us alone."

Unlike conventional ITIs and vocational centers that rely heavily on abstract blackboards, outdated chalk-and-talk lectures, or non-functional mock-up machines, Gram Tarang's pedagogical model is built on three foundational pillars:
1. Production-Floor Immersiveness: Training takes place in authentic, high-value industrial production environments using commercial-grade machinery (5-axis Jyoti CNC machines, Lincoln Electric welding sources, Ashok Leyland BS-VI heavy vehicle bays).
2. Action-Reflection Loop: Rapid feedback loops where trainees physically perform operations, measure dimensional accuracy with micrometers, identify errors, and correct them under master artisan guidance.
3. Commercial Value Validation: Trainees achieve true employability only when they can manufacture parts or deliver services that actual paying commercial clients accept.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: THE FAMOUS 6-STEP TEACHING METHODOLOGY IN DETAIL
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: TEACH ME (Theory, Foundations, Workplace Literacy & Safety)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Provide the conceptual and fundamental trade vocabulary in the trainee's primary regional language (Odia, Telugu, Hindi, Bengali, Assamese).
- Core Curriculum Elements:
  * Technical Terminology & Drawing Literacy: Reading 2D engineering blueprints, isometric projections, circuit schematics, fabric seam diagrams, and diagnostic trouble codes.
  * Workplace Numeracy: Conversions between inches, millimeters, microns; calculating feeds, speeds, gear ratios, electrical resistances, and tolerances.
  * Measurement Science (Metrology): Reading vernier calipers, screw gauges, dial test indicators, bore gauges, and multimeters.
  * Comprehensive Workshop Safety (EHS): 5S methodology (Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain), Personal Protective Equipment (PPE: safety boots, goggles, gloves, ear protection), Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) protocols, and material safety data sheets (MSDS).
- Duration: Typically 10% to 15% of course duration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: SHOW ME (Master Trainer Demonstration & SOP Breakdown)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Visual modeling of excellence by certified master craftsmen and industry experts.
- Execution:
  * Trainees observe master instructors execute complete operational cycles step-by-step.
  * Instructors narrate standard operating procedures (SOPs), pointing out critical safety hazards, tool holding positions, machine sound cues, and chips formation patterns.
  * Complex multi-axis toolpaths and internal engine cycles are demonstrated using 3D visual simulators, interactive touchscreens, and cut-section working models before live machine touch.
- Rule: No trainee operates a powered machine during Step 2; full cognitive attention is dedicated to watching correct technique and posture.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: LET ME (Guided Practice, Simulation & Supervised Runs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Hands-on motor skill acquisition under strict one-on-one supervision.
- Execution:
  * Trainees perform trial runs on non-critical materials (e.g. practicing welds on scrap plates, sewing seams on test swatches, dry-running CNC programs in air above the vice).
  * Instructors intervene immediately upon posture or safety errors to instill muscle memory for correct tool handling and ergonomic body positioning.
  * Trainees learn machine setup, work-holding clamping, zero-point datum setting, tool offsets entry, and program loading.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: ASSESS ME / REVIEW ME (Continuous Formative Evaluation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Immediate, quantitative feedback on accuracy, speed, and quality.
- Execution:
  * Daily and weekly performance evaluations based on objective metrics: dimensional accuracy (within +/- 0.02 mm for CNC machining), cycle time adherence, surface finish (Ra values), and tool wear monitoring.
  * Peer Review: Trainees inspect each other's finished components using quality gauges, learning the mindset of a quality control inspector.
  * Root Cause Analysis: If a job fails inspection, the instructor and trainee analyze whether the root cause was incorrect cutting speed, improper workpiece clamping, or measurement error.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: LET ME SHOW YOU / EMPLOY ME (Work-Integrated Learning Production Shifts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Transition from a trainee into an autonomous, confident industrial producer.
- Execution:
  * Trainees are integrated into Gram Tarang's live Work-Integrated Learning (WEL) production shifts.
  * Working in 8-hour industrial shifts with punch-in/punch-out discipline, trainees manufacture real commercial components for outside corporate clients (Indian Railways, HAL, Ashok Leyland, auto ancillaries).
  * Trainees experience real workplace pressure, quota delivery, quality audits, tool maintenance, and shift handovers.
  * They prove to themselves and visiting recruiters that they can produce commercially acceptable goods on day one of employment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: CERTIFY ME & RECOGNISE ME (Third-Party Assessment & National Honors)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Objective: Independent, uncompromised certification that carries absolute national and international credibility.
- Execution:
  * "Nobody is certified by us alone": Assessments are conducted entirely by external third-party assessors certified by National Sector Skill Councils (SSCs) under NSDC and NCVT.
  * Theory examinations, practical demonstration on machines, and viva voce interviews.
  * Skill Competitions: Outstanding trainees are selected to compete in District, State, Regional, and National Skill Competitions (IndiaSkills) with winners advancing to WorldSkills.
  * Formal Graduation & Rozgar Mela: Certified graduates receive Government of India NSQF certificates and formal appointment letters during campus convocation ceremonies.

SECTION 3: WORK-INTEGRATED LEARNING (WEL) & COMMERCIAL PRODUCTION LABS
================================================================================
A unique hallmark of Gram Tarang is its enterprise-grade production units operating directly inside training campuses.
1. The WEL Model:
- GTET operates functional manufacturing shops that take commercial orders from defense, railways, and automotive OEMs.
- Components Manufactured by Trainees:
  * Precision turned and milled pins, bushes, and brackets for Indian Railways coaches.
  * Precision engine and chassis components for automotive component tiers.
  * Heavy fabrications and welded sub-assemblies for infrastructure projects.
  * School and hospital uniforms stitched under commercial government and institutional orders.
  * Rewinding and maintenance of industrial distribution transformers.
2. Reinvestment in Social Mission:
- Revenues generated from commercial manufacturing in WEL units are directly reinvested into student hostels, subsidizing meals, purchasing state-of-the-art tooling, and funding stipends for rural dropouts.
3. Industry Benefit:
- Employers recruiting from Gram Tarang know that graduates do not require a 6-month probationary training period; they are shop-floor ready on day one.
