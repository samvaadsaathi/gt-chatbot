# GRAM TARANG HISTORY, ENROLMENT TRAJECTORY, FOUNDERS & NATIONAL MILESTONES
# Comprehensive Chronicle of Social Entrepreneurship, Growth Numbers & Recognition (2006–2026)

================================================================================
SECTION 1: FOUNDING ORIGINS & SOCIAL ENTERPRISE MISSION
================================================================================
1. The Vision & Founders:
- Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) was formally incorporated in 2006.
- Co-Founders:
  * Prof. (Dr.) Mukti Kanta Mishra: President of Centurion University and Co-Founder of Gram Tarang. Former corporate executive with multinational banking and hospitality experience who chose to dedicate his life to transformative rural skilling in India.
  * Prof. (Dr.) D.N. Rao: Vice President of Centurion University and Co-Founder of Gram Tarang. Mechanical engineering scholar and entrepreneur visionary committed to building production-grade engineering excellence in tribal heartlands.
- Genesis (1999 to 2006):
  * In the late 1990s, South Odisha (Gajapati and Rayagada districts) was severely underdeveloped, economically impoverished, and afflicted by Left-Wing Extremism (Naxalite insurgency). Youth had few formal livelihood options.
  * In 1999, the founders took over a struggling rural engineering institute (JITM - Jagannath Institute for Technology and Management in Paralakhemundi, Gajapati) and turned it around.
  * Recognising that 90% of rural youth were school dropouts who would never enroll in 4-year engineering degrees, the founders conceptualized Gram Tarang as a dedicated social enterprise in 2006 to impart short-cycle, high-intensity vocational skill training to underprivileged youth.
  * Gram Tarang later birthed Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM) in 2010, India's first multi-sector state-enacted Skill University.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: CHRONOLOGICAL EVOLUTION & EXPANSION TIMELINE
================================================================================
- 2006: Formal incorporation of Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. First residential batches in welding, machining, and industrial stitching launched in Paralakhemundi.
- 2007–2008: Landmark strategic MoU signed with Ashok Leyland to train commercial vehicle technicians. Training extended to auto dealership clusters.
- 2009: Expansion into apparel manufacturing in western Odisha (Bolangir) to mitigate seasonal distress migration of rural families to brick kilns.
- 2010–2011: National Milestone — Gram Tarang becomes the 2nd-ever training partner to be funded and recognized by the newly formed National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) under Prime Minister's National Skill Development Mission.
- 2012: Launch of Café Coffee Day (CCD) pioneer training program for Speech and Hearing Impaired (Deaf & Mute) youth.
- 2013–2014: Accreditation as Project Implementing Agency (PIA) under Ministry of Rural Development's Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY).
- 2015–2016: Massive roll-out of Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 1.0 & 2.0). Establishment of Centers of Excellence with Yamaha and Schneider Electric.
- 2017–2019: Geographic expansion into Andhra Pradesh (Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada), Telangana (Hyderabad), Jharkhand (Jamshedpur), and North-East India (Guwahati, Jorhat).
- 2020–2022: Pandemic resilience: Online-offline blended training, launch of COVID warrior healthcare technician programs (MLT, Emergency Medical Technicians, Patient Care Assistants).
- 2023–2026: Rollout of PMKVY 4.0, NUA Odisha (Industry 4.0 skilling), PM-Vishwakarma traditional artisan missions, EV (Electric Vehicle) servicing, and Drone piloting academies.

SECTION 3: ENROLMENT STATISTICS & MILESTONES
================================================================================
Gram Tarang is recognized as one of India's largest and most impactful vocational training organizations.

CUMULATIVE MILESTONE (LIFE-TO-DATE):
- Total Enrolments & Beneficiaries: OVER 10,14,000 (10.14+ LAKH) YOUTH TRAINED & CERTIFIED.
- Placement & Wage Employment: Over 7,80,000+ candidates successfully linked to formal wage jobs, corporate apprenticeships, or micro-entrepreneurship.
- Women Empowerment Metric: Over 3,80,000+ female trainees trained and placed, predominantly in apparel manufacturing, healthcare, and retail.
- Tribal & Marginalized Communities: Over 55% of all trainees belong to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Minorities, and Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs).

BREAKDOWN BY VERTICAL:
1. Agriculture & Allied RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning): 86,930+ farmers and rural artisans certified.
2. Apparel, Textiles & Garments: 52,628+ women certified and placed in export industries.
3. Manufacturing, CNC Machining & Welding: 52,432+ precision technicians trained.
4. Automotive & Heavy Vehicles (Ashok Leyland, Yamaha, Tata Motors): 26,829+ technicians.
5. Retail, Customer Service & Logistics: 25,136+ associates.
6. Healthcare & Clinical Laboratory Services: 1,300+ technicians.
7. Green Energy, Electrical & Solar PV: 8,400+ electricians and solar technicians.
