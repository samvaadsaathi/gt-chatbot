# GRAM TARANG ADMISSIONS, RESIDENTIAL HOSTELS, FEES & STUDENT WELFARE GUIDE
# Comprehensive Manual for Applicants, Parents, Hostels & Student Life

SECTION 1: ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY & ADMISSION PATHWAYS
================================================================================
Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) provides open, inclusive access to vocational skill training for all young Indians seeking wage employment and formal technical careers.

1. General Eligibility Criteria:
- Minimum Age: 15 to 18 years (depending on trade and scheme).
- Maximum Age:
  * General Government Schemes (PMKVY): Up to 45 years.
  * Rural Livelihood Schemes (DDU-GKY): Up to 35 years for general rural youth; relaxed up to 45 years for Women, SC/ST, and Persons with Disabilities (PwDs).
  * Direct Corporate Sponsorships (Ashok Leyland, Yamaha): 18 to 28 years.
- Educational Qualifications:
  * 5th / 8th Pass: Eligible for Sewing Machine Operator (SMO), Hand Embroidery, Assistant Fitter.
  * 10th Pass (Matriculation): Eligible for CNC Machine Operator, Industrial Electrician, Two-Wheeler Technician, Café Coffee Day Brewmaster, Retail Associate.
  * 12th Pass / Intermediate: Eligible for Medical Laboratory Technician (MLT), Operation Theatre Assistant, Hospitality Front Office, CNC Programmer.
  * ITI Pass / Polytechnic Diploma: Eligible for Advanced Ashok Leyland Diagnostic Technician, 5-Axis CNC Specialist, Robotic Welding Technician.

2. Three Ways to Apply for Admission:
- Pathway A: Walk-in Registration at Any of the 13 Training Centres
  * Applicants visit their nearest Gram Tarang center Monday to Saturday (9:00 AM to 5:30 PM).
  * Professional career counselors evaluate student aptitude, review educational records, and recommend suitable trade options.
- Pathway B: Rural Mobilization & Gram Panchayat Awareness Camps
  * Dedicated field mobilizers visit villages, gram panchayats, and block development offices in coordination with ORMAS, SRLMs, and local community leaders.
  * On-the-spot registration and document verification conducted in native villages.
- Pathway C: Online Registration & AI Inquiry Portal
  * Trainees can apply online via the portal or reach out through email (admissions@gramtarang.org.in / info@gramtarang.org.in) or phone helpline (+91 94386 03040 / +91-674-2386827).

SECTION 2: COMPLETE FEE STRUCTURE & FINANCIAL AID POLICY
================================================================================
Gram Tarang's founding principle is that no willing candidate should be denied technical education due to poverty.

1. Government-Sponsored Schemes (100% Free of Cost):
- Under DDU-GKY (MoRD) and PMKVY 4.0 (MSDE):
  * Tuition Fee: Rs. 0 (100% Free, fully funded by Government of India / State Skill Missions).
  * Admission & Examination Fees: Rs. 0.
  * Study Material, Books & Bag: Rs. 0 (Provided free of charge).
  * Industrial Uniforms & Safety Shoes: Rs. 0 (2 sets of uniforms and 1 pair of certified safety shoes provided free).
  * Residential Hostel & Food: Rs. 0 (100% Free boarding and lodging for residential candidates).
  * Conveyance / Travel Allowance: Provided directly to trainee's bank account for non-residential candidates.

2. Corporate CSR Sponsored Programs (100% Free):
- Programs funded by Ashok Leyland, Gap Inc., Schneider Electric, and Godrej & Boyce are completely free for selected underprivileged candidates.

3. Self-Sponsored / Advanced Modular Courses:
- For candidates who do not qualify for rural BPL government schemes and wish to undergo advanced self-sponsored training (e.g. 5-Axis CNC Programming, Mastercam CAD/CAM):
  * Nominal, highly subsidized course fees (ranging from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 18,000).
  * Flexible installment payment options available.
  * "Earn While You Learn" stipends in Work-Integrated Learning (WEL) production shifts allow candidates to offset their expenses.

SECTION 3: RESIDENTIAL HOSTEL FACILITIES & CAMPUS LIFE
================================================================================
Residential training is a critical component of Gram Tarang's holistic transformation model. Rural youth learn not only technical trade skills, but also workplace hygiene, urban living adaptation, team collaboration, and communicative confidence.

1. Hostel Accommodations:
- Separate Residential Hostels for Boys and Girls at major campuses (Bhubaneswar, Paralakhemundi, Rayagada, Bolangir, Keonjhar).
- Room Setup: Well-ventilated shared dormitories (4 to 6 trainees per room).
- Amenities Included:
  * Individual steel cot/bed with mattress, pillow, and bedsheets.
  * Individual lockable storage wardrobe / trunk.
  * Study table and reading area.
  * Ceiling fans and LED energy-efficient lighting.
  * 24-hour power backup through heavy diesel generators.
  * Pure RO (Reverse Osmosis) drinking water dispensers on every floor.
  * Adequate washroom and bathroom blocks with hot water availability in winter.

2. 24/7 Safety, Security & Surveillance:
- Gated campus with professional security personnel guarding all entry and exit points.
- Biometric attendance systems for tracking morning and evening roll calls.
- High-definition CCTV camera monitoring across corridors, mess halls, and perimeter grounds.
- Resident Wardens living on-premises in both boys' and girls' hostels.
- Special Girls Hostel Protocol: Female security guards, strict visitor registers, and mandatory female warden accompaniment during medical visits.

3. Dining Hall, Nutrition & Mess Menu:
- Wholesome, balanced meals planned to meet nutritional requirements of active young technicians.
- 4-Meal Daily Schedule:
  * 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM: Healthy Breakfast (e.g., Upma, Idli, Puri-Sabzi, boiled egg or banana/milk).
  * 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM: Wholesome Lunch (Steamed rice, dal, seasonal mixed vegetable curry, papad, salad).
  * 05:30 PM – 06:15 PM: Evening Refreshment (Hot tea with biscuits or nutritious snacks).
  * 08:00 PM – 09:15 PM: Wholesome Dinner (Roti/Chapati, rice, dal, vegetable curry; special chicken/egg/paneer meals on designated days).

4. Daily Structured Student Routine:
- 06:00 AM – 07:00 AM: Morning wake-up, physical fitness (PT), yoga, and campus cleanliness.
- 07:30 AM – 08:30 AM: Breakfast and room inspection.
- 09:00 AM – 01:00 PM: Workshop Shift 1 / Technical Theory and Hands-on Machine Operating.
- 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM: Lunch and rest break.
- 02:00 PM – 05:30 PM: Workshop Shift 2 / Practical Simulation, WEL Production Shifts.
- 06:00 PM – 07:30 PM: Soft skills, Spoken English, Digital Literacy (Computer lab), and Financial Literacy.
- 08:00 PM – 09:15 PM: Dinner and social interaction.
- 09:30 PM – 10:00 PM: Self-study and evening roll call.
- 10:15 PM: Lights out and quiet hours.

SECTION 4: MANDATORY DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST FOR ENROLMENT
================================================================================
Candidates must bring original documents for verification along with 3 self-attested photocopies:
1. Aadhaar Card (Must be active and seeded with candidate's mobile number).
2. Educational Qualification Certificates:
   * 10th Mark sheet & Board Pass Certificate (or 8th/12th/ITI certificates as applicable).
   * School Leaving Certificate (SLC) / Transfer Certificate (TC) if available.
3. Proof of Category (For Government Scheme Quota Benefits):
   * Caste Certificate for SC / ST / OBC candidates issued by Revenue Officer (Tahasildar).
   * Disability Certificate issued by District Medical Board (for PwD candidates).
4. Proof of Rural / BPL Status (For DDU-GKY eligibility):
   * BPL Card, Antyodaya Ration Card, or MGNREGA Job Card (with minimum 15 days work record).
5. Bank Account Passbook:
   * Copy of first page showing candidate's name, Bank Account Number, and IFSC code.
   * Bank account must be in candidate's own name and Aadhaar-linked.
6. Photographs: 6 recent passport-size color photographs (white background).
