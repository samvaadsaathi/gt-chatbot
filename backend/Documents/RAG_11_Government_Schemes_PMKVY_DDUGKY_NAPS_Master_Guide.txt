SECTION 1: OVERVIEW OF GRAM TARANG AS IMPLEMENTING PARTNER
================================================================================
Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) is one of India's oldest and highest-rated Social Enterprise Training Partners.
- Empanelled with National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) since 2010 (2nd-ever partner).
- Project Implementing Agency (PIA) for Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD) under DDU-GKY.
- Key Training Partner for Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE) under PMKVY.
- Premier technical partner for Odisha Skill Development Authority (OSDA) under the "Skilled in Odisha" brand.
- Empanelled with State Skill Missions in Andhra Pradesh (APSSDC), Jharkhand (JSDM), Assam (ASDM), Punjab (PSDM), and Chhattisgarh.

Core Principle: Vocational skill training under government schemes at Gram Tarang is 100% FREE OF COST for all eligible youth, including training, study materials, uniform, assessment, certification, and in many schemes, residential accommodation and boarding.

SECTION 2: PRADHAN MANTRI KAUSHAL VIKAS YOJANA (PMKVY 4.0 & SPECIAL PROJECTS)
================================================================================
1. Scheme Identity & Objectives:
- Sponsoring Authority: Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), Government of India.
- Executing Agency: National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) in coordination with State Skill Development Missions (SSDMs).
- Objective: Enable youth to take up industry-relevant skill training that helps them secure a better livelihood.

2. Candidate Eligibility Criteria:
- Nationality: Indian Citizen with valid Aadhaar Card.
- Age Limit: 15 to 45 years.
- Educational Qualification: School dropouts, college dropouts, or unemployed youth possessing minimum qualification specified by National Occupational Standards (NOS) for the chosen job role (ranging from 5th pass for sewing machine operator to 12th/ITI/Diploma for advanced CNC and automotive trades).
- Prior Certification: Candidates must not have undergone training in the same job role under PMKVY previously.

3. Financial Benefits & Fee Structure:
- Course Fee: 100% FREE. The entire training and assessment fee is paid directly by MSDE/NSDC to Gram Tarang.
- Study Materials & Uniform: Provided free of charge to every enrolled trainee.
- Accidental Insurance: Free 3-year accidental insurance cover under Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) with sum insured of Rs. 2,00,000.
- Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT):
  * Conveyance / Travel Allowance for non-residential candidates: Direct bank transfer of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500 per month depending on distance from centre.
  * Post-Placement Support Allowance: Trainees who successfully complete training and get placed receive financial assistance for 2 to 3 months directly in their bank account.

4. PMKVY Components Conducted by Gram Tarang:
- Short Term Training (STT): 200 to 600 hours (2 to 4 months) of intensive hands-on classroom and workshop training. Includes mandatory modules on Soft Skills, Digital Literacy, Financial Literacy, Employability Skills, and Entrepreneurship.
- Special Projects: Tailored interventions for marginalized and vulnerable communities, such as PVTGs (Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups), youth from Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) affected districts of Odisha and Jharkhand, and Persons with Disabilities (PwDs).
- Kaushal Melas & Rozgar Melas: Gram Tarang organizes periodic mobilization camps and job fairs with recruiter partners.

5. Assessment & Certification:
- Conducted by independent third-party assessors certified by respective Sector Skill Councils (e.g., Automotive Skills Development Council - ASDC, Capital Goods Skill Council - CGSC, Apparel Made-Ups & Home Furnishing Sector Skill Council - AMHSSC).
- Successful candidates receive Government of India NSQF (National Skills Qualifications Framework) Level Certificate accessible via Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH) and DigiLocker.

SECTION 3: DEEN DAYAL UPADHYAYA GRAMEEN KAUSHALYA YOJANA (DDU-GKY)
================================================================================
1. Scheme Identity & Focus:
- Sponsoring Authority: Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD), Government of India.
- State Partners: Odisha Rural Development and Marketing Society (ORMAS), APSSDC, JSDM, ASDM.
- Focus: Placement-linked skill training for rural poor youth to diversify rural household incomes and build sustained wage careers.

2. Eligibility Criteria & Social Mandate:
- Target Group: Rural youth from poor households (families possessing BPL Card, SECC 2011 Antyodaya card, RSBY card, or MGNREGA worker card with at least 15 days of work in preceding year).
- Age Limits:
  * General Rural Youth: 15 to 35 years.
  * Women, Persons with Disabilities (PwD), Transgender, and Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTG): Upper age limit relaxed up to 45 years.
- Minimum Education: 8th Pass, 10th Pass, or 12th Pass depending on trade (e.g. 8th pass for Industrial Sewing Machine Operator; 10th pass for Industrial Electrician/Fitter; 12th Science for MLT Healthcare).
- Mandatory Social Inclusion Quotas:
  * Minimum 50% SC and ST candidates.
  * Minimum 15% Minority candidates.
  * Minimum 33% Women candidates across all batches.
  * 3% Persons with Disabilities.

3. Complete Welfare & Free Entitlements:
- Training Fee: 100% Free.
- Residential Hostel Facility: 100% Free boarding and lodging at Gram Tarang residential campuses (e.g., Bhubaneswar, Paralakhemundi, Rayagada).
  * Separate well-secured hostels for male and female trainees with 24/7 warden supervision, CCTV, and biometric attendance.
  * Nutritious 4-time meal schedule: Breakfast with egg/milk, wholesome lunch, evening tea with snacks, and dinner.
- Uniforms & Toolkits: 2 sets of industrial uniforms, 1 pair of safety shoes, safety goggles, notebook, and curriculum handbook provided free of cost.
- Medical & Health: Regular health check-ups and doctor on call at residential centres.
- Tablet / IT Lab Access: Continuous access to digital learning facilities and computer literacy labs.

4. Financial Support & Post-Placement Support (PPS):
- Residential Trainees: Free boarding and lodging during the entire course duration.
- Non-Residential Trainees: Direct transport allowance of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500/month credited to Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- Post-Placement Support (PPS Allowance): To support the transition from village to industrial cities (e.g., Pune, Chennai, Bengaluru, Tirupur):
  * Placement within district of domicile: Rs. 1,000/month for 2 months.
  * Placement within state of domicile (different district): Rs. 1,000/month for 3 months.
  * Placement outside state of domicile (Inter-state placement): Rs. 1,000/month for 6 months (Total Rs. 6,000 directly credited).

5. Placement Mandate & Retention Standards:
- Mandatory Placement: DDU-GKY guidelines mandate that at least 70% of certified trainees must be placed in formal wage employment.
- Minimum Wage Guarantee: All placed candidates receive statutory minimum wages or higher (typically Rs. 12,500 to Rs. 22,000 per month).
- Formal Sector Social Security: Mandatory enrollment in Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).
- Post-Placement Tracking: Gram Tarang dedicated placement tracking officers follow up with each candidate and employer for 12 months after placement to ensure career retention, lodging assistance, and dispute resolution.

SECTION 4: NATIONAL APPRENTICESHIP PROMOTION SCHEME (NAPS & WISTA)
================================================================================
1. Objective:
- Promote apprenticeship training in manufacturing and engineering industries, giving trainees shop-floor experience while earning a living ("Earn While You Learn").

2. Program Structure:
- Duration: 12 to 24 Months.
- Locations: Live production facilities of corporate partners (Ashok Leyland plants at Pantnagar/Hosur/Alwar, Tata Motors at Pune/Jamshedpur, Godrej & Boyce at Mumbai, SEDA, Schneider Electric).
- Work-Study Balance: Trainees spend practical time on the industrial shop-floor manufacturing commercial goods under the guidance of certified shop supervisors, alongside weekly classroom theory modules by Gram Tarang.

3. Monthly Stipend & Financials:
- Every apprentice receives a monthly stipend directly into their bank account ranging from Rs. 8,500 to Rs. 15,000/month depending on trade and location.
- Government of India directly reimburses 25% of prescribed stipend up to Rs. 1,500 per month per apprentice to employers through DBT.
- Subsidized food and factory transport provided by host corporations.

4. Outcome & Certification:
- Trainees appear for National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) examination conducted by National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT).
- NAC certificate holders qualify for permanent technical positions in PSUs (Railways, BHEL, SAIL, HAL) and private OEMs with higher pay scales.

SECTION 6: RECOGNITION OF PRIOR LEARNING (RPL)
================================================================================
1. Purpose: Formally assess, validate, and certify skills acquired informally through on-the-job experience without formal certificates.
2. Target Beneficiaries: Farm workers, construction artisans, carpenters, garment tailors, mechanics, retail associates with 1+ years experience.
3. Gram Tarang Achievements:
- Over 86,930 farmers and agricultural artisans certified under Agriculture Sector Skill Council.
- Tailors and garment stitchers certified across rural clusters.
4. Process:
- 12-hour mandatory orientation (soft skills, digital transactions, health and safety standards).
- Practical assessment on tools.
- Trainees receive: Skill Certificate, NSQF ID Card, accidental insurance of Rs. 2 Lakh, and Rs. 500 reward via DBT.

SECTION 7: PM-VISHWAKARMA YOJANA & TRADITIONAL ARTISAN SUPPORT
================================================================================
1. Overview: Launched by Central Government to uplift traditional craftsmen and artisans.
2. 18 Traditional Trades Covered: Carpenter (Suthar), Boat Maker, Armourer, Blacksmith (Lohar), Hammer and Tool Kit Maker, Locksmith, Sculptor, Goldsmith, Potter, Cobbler, Mason (Rajmistri), Basket/Mat/Broom Maker, Doll & Toy Maker, Barber, Garland Maker, Washerman (Dhobi), Tailor (Darzi), and Fishing Net Maker.
3. Gram Tarang Training Role:
- Basic Skill Training (5 to 7 days) and Advanced Skill Training (15 days).
- Training Stipend: Rs. 500 per day credited directly to artisan's bank account.
- Modern Tool-Kit Incentive: Rs. 15,000 digital voucher for purchasing modern trade equipment.
- Collateral-Free Credit Support: Enterprise loans up to Rs. 1,00,000 (Tranche 1) and Rs. 2,00,000 (Tranche 2) at concessional 5% interest rate.
