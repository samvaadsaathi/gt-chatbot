CATEGORY 1: INSTITUTIONAL BACKGROUND, FOUNDERS & ACCREDITATIONS
================================================================================

Q: What is Gram Tarang Employability Training Services?
A: Gram Tarang Employability Training Services Pvt. Ltd. (GTET) is a premier social enterprise incorporated in 2006, originating from Centurion University of Technology and Management roots established in 1999. It provides vocational skill training, workforce solutions, and wage employment to youth across India, particularly in tribal, rural, and underserved areas.

Q: Who are the co-founders of Gram Tarang?
A: Gram Tarang was co-founded by Prof. (Dr.) Mukti Kanta Mishra (President of Centurion University) and Prof. (Dr.) D.N. Rao (Vice President of Centurion University). Both left prestigious corporate and academic careers to establish high-precision vocational training in tribal South Odisha.

Q: How is Gram Tarang connected to Centurion University?
A: Gram Tarang operates as the social enterprise skill training arm of the Centurion University Group. Its central Mother Campuses are integrated within Centurion University campuses (such as Bhubaneswar and Paralakhemundi), giving vocational trainees access to university-grade labs, 5-axis CNC machines, and residential infrastructure.

Q: Is Gram Tarang accredited by NSDC and Government of India?
A: Yes. Gram Tarang was the 2nd-ever training partner funded and empanelled by the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC) in 2010. It is an accredited Project Implementing Agency (PIA) under MoRD for DDU-GKY, a key training partner under MSDE for PMKVY, and the technical partner for Odisha Skill Development Authority (OSDA).

Q: How many students has Gram Tarang trained life-to-date?
A: Life-to-date, Gram Tarang has enrolled and certified over 10,14,000 (10.14+ Lakh) youth across India, successfully placing over 7,80,000+ candidates in formal wage jobs and formal sector apprenticeships.

CATEGORY 2: GOVERNMENT SCHEMES & FINANCIAL BENEFITS
================================================================================

Q: Are training courses at Gram Tarang free of cost?
A: YES. Under government-sponsored schemes such as DDU-GKY (MoRD) and PMKVY 4.0 (MSDE), training is 100% FREE OF COST for eligible candidates. This includes free tuition, free study materials, free industrial uniforms, free safety shoes, free examination/certification, and free residential boarding and lodging in hostels.

Q: What are the eligibility criteria for PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana)?
A: To join PMKVY at Gram Tarang:
- Must be an Indian citizen with an Aadhaar card.
- Age between 15 and 45 years.
- School dropouts, college dropouts, or unemployed youth.
- Minimum educational criteria depends on the trade (from 5th pass for sewing machine operator to 10th/12th/ITI for CNC and automotive trades).

Q: What are the eligibility criteria for DDU-GKY (Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana)?
A: For DDU-GKY:
- Must belong to a rural poor household (possessing BPL card, Antyodaya ration card, SECC 2011 status, or MGNREGA worker card with at least 15 days of work in preceding year).
- Age limit: 15 to 35 years for general rural youth.
- Upper age limit relaxed up to 45 years for Women, SC/ST candidates, Persons with Disabilities (PwDs), and Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs).
- Minimum education: 8th pass, 10th pass, or 12th pass depending on the trade.

Q: Do trainees receive any stipend or financial allowances during and after training?
A: Yes:
- Under DDU-GKY:
  * Residential trainees receive 100% free boarding and lodging.
  * Non-residential trainees receive a direct transport allowance of Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 1,500/month in their bank account.
  * Post-Placement Support (PPS): Candidates placed outside their home state receive Rs. 1,000/month for 6 months (total Rs. 6,000) directly into their bank account to help settle in destination cities. Placed candidates within state receive Rs. 1,000/month for 3 months.
- Under NAPS (National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme):
  * Apprentices earn a monthly stipend of Rs. 8,500 to Rs. 15,000/month while working on company shop floors.
- Under PM-Vishwakarma:
  * Traditional artisans receive a training stipend of Rs. 500 per day plus a Rs. 15,000 modern toolkit voucher.

Q: What is the "Sudakshya" scheme in Odisha?
A: Sudakshya is an Odisha Skill Development Authority (OSDA) initiative implemented by Gram Tarang to encourage girls in technical education. Female trainees receive 100% admission fee waivers, free hostel facilities, free uniforms, and a monthly maintenance stipend of Rs. 1,000.

CATEGORY 3: HOSTELS, MEALS & STUDENT WELFARE
================================================================================

Q: Does Gram Tarang provide hostel facilities for students?
A: Yes. Gram Tarang operates large, modern residential hostels with separate wings for male and female trainees at its major campuses (Bhubaneswar, Paralakhemundi, Rayagada, Bolangir, Keonjhar).

Q: Are the hostels safe for female candidates?
A: Absolutely. Girls' hostels feature 24/7 security with dedicated female security guards, biometric attendance systems, CCTV monitoring on perimeter and gates, resident female wardens, and strict visitor protocols.

Q: What kind of food is served in the hostel mess?
A: Trainees receive 4 hygienic, wholesome meals daily:
- Breakfast (07:30 AM – 08:30 AM): Upma, Idli, Puri-Sabzi, boiled egg or milk/banana.
- Lunch (01:00 PM – 02:00 PM): Steamed rice, dal, mixed seasonal vegetable curry, papad, salad.
- Evening Refreshment (05:30 PM – 06:15 PM): Tea with biscuits or hot snacks.
- Dinner (08:00 PM – 09:15 PM): Roti, rice, dal, vegetable curry; special chicken/egg/paneer dishes on designated days.

Q: What amenities are provided in the hostel rooms?
A: Trainees receive an individual steel cot with mattress and bedsheets, personal lockable storage wardrobe, ceiling fans, LED lighting, 24-hour backup generator power, RO purified drinking water stations, and clean washroom blocks.

CATEGORY 4: SKILL TRAINING COURSES, TRADES & CURRICULUM
================================================================================

Q: What skill programs are offered in Manufacturing?
A: CNC Operator (Turning & Milling, 3 months), CNC Programmer / CAD-CAM (4 months), Industrial Fitter (3 months), MIG/MAG and TIG Welder (3 months), Tool & Die Maker.

Q: What is the Ashok Leyland Commercial Vehicle Technician course?
A: A 4-month residential joint academy program co-designed with Ashok Leyland. Trainees learn heavy BS-VI diesel engine overhauls, common rail injection (CRDI), air brake systems, ABS, and i-Gen6 electronic diagnostics on full Ashok Leyland heavy chassis. Certified graduates receive 100% placement linkage across Ashok Leyland's 300+ dealerships in India.

Q: What is the Yamaha Training Academy?
A: A 3-month two-wheeler technical specialist course run in co-branded Yamaha bays, training students on four-stroke motorcycle engines, fuel injection (FI) diagnostic tools, and electrical schematics, with placement across authorized Yamaha dealerships.

Q: What is the Café Coffee Day Brewmaster program for deaf and mute youth?
A: Gram Tarang and CCD pioneered an inclusive "Silent Brewmasters" program where Speech and Hearing Impaired (Deaf & Mute) youth are trained by Indian Sign Language (ISL) certified master trainers in coffee brewing, espresso calibration, milk frothing, and POS billing. Over 450+ deaf and mute youth have been successfully placed in CCD outlets across India.

Q: What trades are available for women with low educational qualifications?
A: Industrial Sewing Machine Operator (SMO, 2-3 months) for 5th/8th pass women, with integrated Gap Inc. P.A.C.E. life-skills. Leads to guaranteed formal wage employment in export garment factories in Bengaluru, Tirupur, and Chennai. Also Healthcare Assistants and Retail Sales Associates.

Q: What is the 6-Step Teaching Methodology?
A: The 6 steps are:
1. TEACH ME: Technical theory, measurement science, blueprints, and 5S workshop safety.
2. SHOW ME: Master trainer visual demonstration of standard operating procedures (SOPs).
3. LET ME: Guided, supervised hands-on trial runs on machines.
4. ASSESS ME / REVIEW ME: Daily quantitative inspection of dimensional precision and cycle times.
5. LET ME SHOW YOU: Work-Integrated Learning (WEL) live commercial production shifts.
6. CERTIFY ME & RECOGNISE ME: Independent third-party Sector Skill Council assessment and NSQF certification.

Q: What is Gram Tarang's placement record?
A: Gram Tarang maintains an audited 78% to 82% placement offer rate across all completed batches.

Q: Which companies recruit Gram Tarang graduates?
A: Key corporate recruiters include: Ashok Leyland, Tata Motors, Yamaha Motor India, Café Coffee Day, Schneider Electric, Godrej & Boyce, Sansera Engineering, SEDA, Shahi Exports, Arvind Mills, Gokaldas Exports, Apollo Hospitals, Reliance Retail, and DMart.

Q: What is the starting salary for placed graduates?
A: Starting monthly salaries range from Rs. 12,500 to Rs. 24,000 per month depending on the trade, location, and educational qualification. In addition, 100% of jobs include statutory Provident Fund (EPF) and Health Insurance (ESIC), and many employers provide subsidized hostel accommodation and meals.

Q: Does Gram Tarang support students after they join a job?
A: Yes. Gram Tarang maintains dedicated Post-Placement Tracking Officers (PPTO) who track and mentor each placed student for 6 to 12 months, assisting with city transition, safe hostel accommodations, bank remittances, and workplace grievance resolution.
